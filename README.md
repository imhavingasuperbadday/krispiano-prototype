# Kris Piano Songs — prototype

The piano alone on black, the framing of the *Kris Piano Songs* title card,
rebuilt as a playable scene. Open `index.html` (double-click works; no server
needed).

The earlier version — the one set in Noelle's house, with the room, the
dialogue box and the room music — is kept intact in
[`noelle_house_version/`](noelle_house_version/) and is worked on separately.

## Controls

| Key | |
|---|---|
| Arrows / WASD | walk |
| Shift or X | run |
| Z / Enter / Space | confirm |
| Z at the keys | open the song list — then a song, or play it yourself |

Sitting at the piano:

| Key | |
|---|---|
| X | stand up |
| C | pick something else without standing |
| L | loop the current song |
| W | start / stop recording |
| R | drop your recording for this song |
| Esc | stand up (works even while the keys are live) |
| **Z X C V B N** | left hand, outermost to innermost |
| **P O I U Y T** | right hand, outermost to innermost |

## What it is

**The stage** is nothing at all: black, with the grand piano and its bench
(`spr_noellehouse_kitchen_piano`, 89×95, and `..._piano_seat`, 46×19) centred
on it, drawn at 2× in the 640×360 view — the size and framing the title card
uses. The stage is exactly one screen wide, so the camera never moves and the
sprites keep their native pixels. Kris starts to the left of the piano and
walks the whole stage; the piano is the only thing he can't walk through.

Both sprites carried a patch of the kitchen floor baked in behind the legs, so
those pixels (`#deBB9c`) are cleared to transparency here — on black the piano
would otherwise sit on a peach-coloured slab. The originals are untouched in
`noelle_house_version/assets/`.

There is no dialogue box: facing the keys and pressing `Z` opens the song list
straight away. Nothing is drawn over the key band while Kris plays either; the
struck-key shading read as dark specks on the keys and is gone.

### LOWER plays the way the game plays it

`spr_kris_piano_full` is a 525-frame animation of Kris from behind at the keys,
and it was drawn for **one track**. `obj_ch4_DCA09_piano` doesn't animate it on
a timer — it reads the frame straight off the song's playhead:

```gml
var track_progress = audio_sound_get_track_position(piano_song[1]);
var track_progress_normal = (track_progress - 0) / (audio_sound_length(piano_song[1]) - 0.75 - 0);
kris_marker.image_index = 524 * track_progress_normal;
```

That runs verbatim here for **LOWER** (`kris_piano_lower.ogg`), the track the
object actually plays, and LOWER never uses anything else — that animation is
its animation. It's marked `+` in the song list.

### The other eight use Toby's puppet

The other tracks were never animated, and stretching LOWER's performance over
them is just wrong. Chapter 4 contains the real answer: **`obj_kris_pianopuppet`**,
an authoring tool left in the build, belonging to no room. It isn't a baked
animation — it's a rig:

- `spr_kris_pianopuppet_body`, plus `_larm` and `_rarm` at 12 frames each
- six hand positions along the keyboard × a raised and a key-down pose, indexed
  `baselindex + (lkeydown * 6)`
- left hand on `Z X C V B N`, right hand on `P O I U Y T`
- and it **records what you play**, one character per frame:
  `1 2 3 L U R` for the six positions, `W` for a rest

There's even a real take hardcoded in it — 257 frames, about 8.6 seconds of
two-handed playing. (The arm sprites also carry the artist's own `L1..L12` /
`R1..R12` labels baked into their top rows; those are stripped here.)

So each of the eight gets a performance in exactly that format, generated by
`tools/make_performances.py`: short-time spectra at 30fps, split at C4 into a
low band for the left hand and a high band for the right, spectral flux for the
note onsets, and a harmonic-sum pitch estimate at each onset deciding which of
the six positions the hand moves to. Between onsets the hand holds its place
with the arm raised, exactly as the tool's playback does.

### And you can record your own

Sit down, pick **PLAY IT YOURSELF**, and the twelve keys are live — Kris plays
what you play, with `snd_krispiano` repitched per key. Or pick a song, hit `W`,
and play along with it: the recording starts the track from the top and saves
when you press `W` again. A saved performance replaces the generated one for
that song and is marked `R` in the list; `R` while seated drops it again.
Recordings live in browser storage and are the same kind of string the
generated ones are, so the two are interchangeable.

To regenerate the automatic performances after changing anything:

```
python tools/make_performances.py     # needs ffmpeg and numpy
```

### Everything else

Both rigs are **dark-world** sprites — three colours, navy / blue / pale.
Settings → *Piano Kris* switches between them as decompiled and a light-world
recolour that maps those three onto the ones `spr_krisd` uses. Settings →
*Animation* switches the eight between the puppet and the old stretched-baked
behaviour, for comparison; LOWER ignores it.

**Movement** is the `obj_mainchara/Step_0.gml` port from the Egg Room
prototype — run boost, facing latch, wall-slide and corner resolution, with
every 30 fps constant halved for 60 fps and normalised for any refresh rate.
Settings has the old 8-way walker too.

### The camera doesn't move

`obj_mainchara/Step_2.gml`, light-world branch, has no easing in it — every
step the view is *assigned* and then clamped to the room. Here there is nothing
to clamp against and nothing to follow: the stage is one screen, so the view is
nailed to its origin at 1:1 and the piano stays where it is drawn.

### The interface

The title screen, settings, song list and piano HUD are the prototype's own
plain styling, drawn at 1x over the 2x stage. `drawText` tints `fnt_main`
(8bitoperator JVE) per colour on demand.

## Songs

All nine piano tracks from `mus/kris_piano/`:

LOWER · PROPHECY · LAST PROPHECY · LANCER'S WALTZ · ROUXLS KAARD · SHOP ·
WAITING ROOM · SEVEN / FOUR · QUIZ

Nothing else sounds: the room ambience (`room_noelle_house.ogg`) belonged to
the house and is gone from this version. It is still in
`noelle_house_version/assets/mus/`.

## Debug

`index.html?auto=1` drops straight onto the stage. `&x=`/`&y=` place Kris,
`&seat=N` sits him at song N, `&free=1` starts free play, `&picker=N` opens the
song list, `&pt=SEC` poses the puppet from that song's performance, `&rec=N`
shows the recording HUD, `&menu=N` opens settings, `&mute=1` starts silent
(`&frame=N` freezes a baked animation frame). Handy for screenshots.

```
assets/

  kris/{d,u,l,r}_{0..3}.png    spr_krisd / spr_krisu / spr_krisl / spr_krisr
  piano_full.png               spr_kris_piano_full, 525 frames, 25x21 grid of 52px cells
  piano_full_lw.png            ...recoloured to the light-world palette
  puppet_body.png              spr_kris_pianopuppet_body
  puppet_larm.png              spr_kris_pianopuppet_larm, 12 frames
  puppet_rarm.png              spr_kris_pianopuppet_rarm, 12 frames
  puppet_*_lw.png              ...recoloured
  performances.js              one performance string per hand per song
  fnt_main.png  heart.png (spr_heartsmall)
  mus/kris_piano_*.ogg         the nine piano tracks
  sfx/*.wav                    sfx/krispiano.wav is snd_krispiano, the note
tools/
  make_performances.py         regenerates assets/performances.js
noelle_house_version/          the previous version, room and all, untouched
```

The room art (`room_bg.png`, `tree.png`, `chairs.png`, `door_closed.png`,
`piano_keys.png`) and the room music are no longer loaded here; they live on in
`noelle_house_version/assets/`.
